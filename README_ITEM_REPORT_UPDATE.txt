MY LEDGER v1.3.0 - ITEM & DATE-WISE REPORT UPDATE

Added:
1. Inventory item catalog for each shop.
2. Any item used in an inventory entry is automatically saved to the item dropdown.
3. Settings > Items, Price & Unit page lets the owner add, edit and delete items.
4. Each saved item stores Item Name, Unit and Default Rate.
5. Selecting a saved item in a new entry automatically fills Unit and Rate.
6. Inventory line amount is automatic: Quantity × Rate.
7. Tally lock remains active: transaction cannot save until Entry Amount equals Inventory Total exactly.
8. Reports are date-wise with Day, Week, Month, All, From Date and To Date.
9. ITEM SUMMARY now keeps entries exactly as entered, e.g.
   Kishor 4 Pc × 5 = ₹20
   Chini 1 Kg × 70 = ₹70
   Shampoo 5 Pc × 1 = ₹5
   Kamla 1 Pc × 5 = ₹5
   Total = ₹100
10. The same exact item-line format is used in the generated PDF report.
