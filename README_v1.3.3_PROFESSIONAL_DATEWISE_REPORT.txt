MY LEDGER v1.3.3 – PROFESSIONAL DATE-WISE REPORT

Report format:
- Business name, address, mobile and email header
- Date-wise transaction groups
- Item lines exactly as entered: Item Qty Unit × Rate = Amount
- Transaction/customer name above each entry
- Received Payment lines for credit entries
- Running Balance after each entry
- Opening balance for each date group
- Total Debit, Total Credit and FINAL BALANCE with DR/CR
- PDF and in-app report use date-wise item/payment presentation

Example:
01 Sep 2026
Kishor 4 Pc × 5 = ₹20
Chini 1 Kg × 70 = ₹70
Shampoo 5 Pc × 1 = ₹5
Kamla 1 Pc × 5 = ₹5
Total = ₹100

02 Sep 2026
Sugar 2 Kg × 50 = ₹100
Oil 1 Ltr × 120 = ₹120
Total = ₹220

Received Payment = ₹200
Running Balance = ₹120 DR

FINAL BALANCE = ₹120 DR
